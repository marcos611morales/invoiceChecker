## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** organizar el gasto en mis propias categorías (por proveedor y por concepto), que persisten en el navegador y se pueden respaldar, **so that** veo "en qué gastamos más" por categoría y me llevo el análisis filtrado a un Excel de varias hojas.

(User story restated from the ROADMAP Phase 3 Goal line without adding content; the ROADMAP line itself is unchanged.)

<objective>
Deliver the Phase 3 tracer: one real path from the user's saved category settings in the browser, through the persisted settings store (version 2, migrated from the Phase 2 contract), the category resolver and the per-concept split of the pure accounting layer, to a «Gasto por categoría» block on the Dashboard whose rows add up to «Gasto total».

Purpose: prove the riskiest seams of the phase end to end first (the one-way stored format and its migration, D-06 and D-25; the split identity, D-13; the block next to the ranking, D-16) before Plans 03-02 to 03-17 deepen it, and record the two blocking human gates of the phase first: the package approval for the Excel library (installed in Plan 03-13) and the one-way choice of the stored and backup format (D-06, D-07). The entry point of this slice is the stored settings themselves; Plan 03-02 adds the Categorías tab (D-01) that writes them from the UI.
Output: the approved package list, the chosen format, the settings model (`src/domain/settings` types, schema, migration, resolver), `splitEntry` and `categoryBreakdown`, the version-2 settings store, the «Gasto por categoría» block in the Q1 layout, and an end-to-end component test.
</objective>
