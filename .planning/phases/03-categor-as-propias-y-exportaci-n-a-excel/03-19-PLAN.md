## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** organizar el gasto en mis propias categorías (por proveedor y por concepto), que persisten en el navegador y se pueden respaldar, **so that** veo "en qué gastamos más" por categoría y me llevo el análisis filtrado a un Excel de varias hojas.

<objective>
Close UAT gap G-03-7-WR02 (03-REVIEW.md WR-02). The user's triage decision is final: disclose the rounding, do not write unrounded values.

Each Monto cell of Facturas and Conceptos is rounded to cents. The Dashboard, the «Gasto total» of Parámetros and the Total rows of the summary sheets round the exact sum once. So when an amount has more than two decimals, the sum of a column can differ by up to half a cent per invoice. D-20 says the column «cuadra» with the Dashboard, and nothing in the file explains the difference.

The fix has three parts: a note in the Parámetros sheet (through `es.export.params.noteText`), a test that pins the note, and a record of the decision in D-20.

The exact Spanish text is UI copy, and the project rule (CLAUDE.md rule 1) forbids inventing it. The user has already confirmed it (see «Resolved decision» below), so this plan has no checkpoint and runs autonomously.

Purpose: XLS-03 (the totals of the Excel match the Dashboard, and the file says when cents can differ) and XLS-01 (Parámetros explains how the file was produced).
Output: the confirmed copy in es.ts, the note in buildParametros with its test, D-20 amended in 03-CONTEXT.md and the Parámetros line of the 03-UI-SPEC.md workbook contract updated.
