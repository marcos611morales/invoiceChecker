## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** organizar el gasto en mis propias categorías (por proveedor y por concepto), que persisten en el navegador y se pueden respaldar, **so that** veo "en qué gastamos más" por categoría y me llevo el análisis filtrado a un Excel de varias hojas.

<objective>
Close UAT gap G-03-7-WR01 (03-REVIEW.md WR-01, user triage 2026-10-18: fix now). «Subir ajustes» swaps the category list but leaves the old ids in the session category filter. The figures ignore them (selectView applies effectiveSelection), but the FilterBar chip and the «Categorías» row of the exported Parámetros sheet print them as raw ids, so the file misstates how it was produced.

Fix it at the source and at both readers. The confirm handler prunes the session filter to the categories that exist after the replace (SIN_CATEGORIA stays). FilterBar and ExportButton read the effective selection, so no other path can bring a raw id back on screen or into the file.

Purpose: FILT-03 (the chip tells the truth about the category filter), AJUS-02 (restoring settings leaves a coherent session), XLS-01 and XLS-02 (Parámetros reproduces the filters that produced the file).
Output: `effectiveSelection` exported from view.ts, the `pruneCategoryFilter` store action wired into the confirm handler, FilterBar and ExportButton on the effective selection, and regression tests through the whole shell (upload, confirm, chip, export).
</objective>
