## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** ver para cada producto agrupado en qué tienda me sale más barato, cuánto podría ahorrar y cómo ha cambiado su precio en el tiempo, comparando solo cantidades comparables, **so that** sepamos dónde nos conviene comprar cada producto sin subir los datos a ningún servidor.

<objective>
Keep services, fuel and commissions out of the comparison by default and give the user's per-product choice a consistent data path (PREC-03): the ClaveProdServ classification, the default computed over the unfiltered purchases, the savings summary over comparable products only, the stored overrides with undo, and consistency with product deletion.

Purpose: products like gasoline or Google Ads may have been grouped in Phase 4; comparing their «unit price» is meaningless and would distort the savings total. After this plan the card already leaves them out; Plan 05-09 adds the row menu, the «Mostrar los no comparables» checkbox and the notices on top of this data path.
Output: `comparable.ts`, `summarizeSavings`, comparability in `usePricesView`, the override reducers in `settings/prices.ts`, product deletion carrying overrides, the store actions and their tests.
</objective>
