## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** ver para cada producto agrupado en qué tienda me sale más barato, cuánto podría ahorrar y cómo ha cambiado su precio en el tiempo, comparando solo cantidades comparables, **so that** sepamos dónde nos conviene comprar cada producto sin subir los datos a ningún servidor.

<objective>
Read presentation sizes from the descriptions of piece-unit purchases (D-09 step 3) and piece counts (D-25), conservatively, and plug the reader into content resolution, so Walmart-style lines («12/1L ALPURA», «2KG MANGO MM») compare per kg or L instead of per piece.

Purpose: on the real sample only about one in five piece-unit members carries a readable size; the reader must find those without ever producing a wrong magnitude (×1000 thousands-dot trap, ×24 pack trap). Everything it cannot read falls back to D-10 and to the content editor of Plan 05-11.
Output: `sizeReader.ts` with its contract table, the piece branch of `resolveContent`, an anonymized fixture test and a real-data coverage test.
</objective>
