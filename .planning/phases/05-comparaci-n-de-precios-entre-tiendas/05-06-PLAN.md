## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** ver para cada producto agrupado en qué tienda me sale más barato, cuánto podría ahorrar y cómo ha cambiado su precio en el tiempo, comparando solo cantidades comparables, **so that** sepamos dónde nos conviene comprar cada producto sin subir los datos a ningún servidor.

<objective>
Show how a product's price changed over time (PREC-06, D-24): a per-store chart of normalized prices by purchase date inside the opened product, with stable colors and shapes per store, a legend, per-mark tooltips and the accessible text the UI-SPEC requires.

Purpose: the per-store table says where it is cheapest now; the history shows whether that is a trend or a single lucky purchase.
Output: `history.ts` (pure), stable slots in `usePricesView`, `HistoryChart.tsx`, six series tokens in `globals.css`, the panel wiring, copy and date helpers.

Planning finding that needs the user: Recharts 3.8.0's LineChart accepts only axis tooltips (node_modules/recharts/es6/chart/LineChart.js: allowedTooltipTypes ['axis']), while UI-SPEC asks for a LineChart with a per-mark tooltip (shared false). ScatterChart allows only item tooltips and its Scatter accepts line, lineType "joint" and a custom shape. Task 1 asks which way to resolve the conflict before the chart is built.
</objective>
