## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** ver para cada producto agrupado en qué tienda me sale más barato, cuánto podría ahorrar y cómo ha cambiado su precio en el tiempo, comparando solo cantidades comparables, **so that** sepamos dónde nos conviene comprar cada producto sin subir los datos a ningún servidor.

<objective>
Open a product to see why it is priced the way it is: the summary with the cheapest store and the saving explained, the per-store table (minimum, median, last, purchases) with the cheapest store highlighted, the notes for ties, mixed families and per-piece comparisons, and the purchases list with each purchase's content, unit price or reason for not being normalized.

Purpose: this is where PREC-01 is fully visible (per store, not only the cheapest) and where a user can audit a strange price (D-08). The chart (Plan 05-07) and the content editor (Plan 05-11) slot into this panel.
Output: `ProductDetail.tsx`, `StoreTable.tsx`, `PurchasesList.tsx`, the expander in `PricesTable.tsx`, copy and format helpers, tests.
</objective>
