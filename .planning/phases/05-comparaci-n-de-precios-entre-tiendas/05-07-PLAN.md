## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** ver para cada producto agrupado en qué tienda me sale más barato, cuánto podría ahorrar y cómo ha cambiado su precio en el tiempo, comparando solo cantidades comparables, **so that** sepamos dónde nos conviene comprar cada producto sin subir los datos a ningún servidor.

<objective>
Keep services, fuel and commissions out of the comparison by default and let the user change it per product (PREC-03): the ClaveProdServ classification, the default computed over the unfiltered purchases, the stored overrides with undo, consistency with product deletion, the row menu, the «Mostrar los no comparables» checkbox and the related empty state and notices.

Purpose: products like gasoline or Google Ads may have been grouped in Phase 4; comparing their «unit price» is meaningless and would distort the savings total.
Output: `comparable.ts`, `settings/prices.ts` override reducers, product deletion carrying overrides, store actions, the tab UI, copy and tests.
</objective>
