## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** ver para cada producto agrupado en qué tienda me sale más barato, cuánto podría ahorrar y cómo ha cambiado su precio en el tiempo, comparando solo cantidades comparables, **so that** sepamos dónde nos conviene comprar cada producto sin subir los datos a ningún servidor.

<objective>
Complete the comparison rules the tracer wired, and pin every one of them with a test table: which lines are purchases in each IVA mode, the SAT unit table and content reasons, the per-piece and mixed-family decisions, the per-store statistics, the ties of the cheapest store, the saving and the savings summary.

Purpose: after this plan every figure the Precios tab shows (price, cheapest store, saving, total) follows the user's rules in the edge cases too (ties, single purchases, credit notes, $0 lines, mixed units), with exact big.js results. It also creates the shared test builders the later plans use.
Output: `tests/support/pricesFactory.ts`, completed `content.ts` and `compare.ts`, and the four domain test files.
</objective>
