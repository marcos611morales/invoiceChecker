## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** ver para cada producto agrupado en qué tienda me sale más barato, cuánto podría ahorrar y cómo ha cambiado su precio en el tiempo, comparando solo cantidades comparables, **so that** sepamos dónde nos conviene comprar cada producto sin subir los datos a ningún servidor.

<objective>
Expand the tracer's tab into the full UI-SPEC composition: global filters and mode captions, the complete savings card, the tools row (search and count line), the two empty states of this slice, and the products table with sorting, pagination, badges and every cell state.

Purpose: after this plan a user can find any grouped product, sort by saving, see which comparisons are per piece or partly not normalized, and recover from an empty filter result, all without leaving the tab.
Output: completed `PreciosTab.tsx` and `PricesTable.tsx`, their tests, the copy and the format helpers.
</objective>
