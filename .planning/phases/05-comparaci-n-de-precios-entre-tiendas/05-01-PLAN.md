## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** ver para cada producto agrupado en qué tienda me sale más barato, cuánto podría ahorrar y cómo ha cambiado su precio en el tiempo, comparando solo cantidades comparables, **so that** sepamos dónde nos conviene comprar cada producto sin subir los datos a ningún servidor.

<objective>
Tracer of the phase: wire one path from the loaded invoices to a new «Precios» tab, through every layer this phase touches on the read side (pure price domain, the view hook, the tab panel and its table, the shell), and then let the six-tab list scroll on tablets and phones.

Purpose: prove the architecture on the first commit: the filtered, category-scoped ledger entries the Dashboard already uses (FILT-05) become purchases of accepted products, each purchase gets a content in kg, L or pieces from the SAT unit, and the comparison (median per store, cheapest store, estimated saving) reaches the screen. The comparison rules and their test table (Plan 05-03), the table tools (05-04), the size reader (05-05), the expanded panel (05-06), the chart (05-07), the «no comparable» mark (05-08, 05-09) and the content editor (05-10, 05-11) all plug into the interfaces fixed here without an architectural change.
Output: `src/domain/prices/{units,content,purchases,compare}.ts`, `usePricesView`, `PreciosTab`, `PricesTable`, the sixth tab with its tests, and the copy.
</objective>
