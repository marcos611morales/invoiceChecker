## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** ver para cada producto agrupado en qué tienda me sale más barato, cuánto podría ahorrar y cómo ha cambiado su precio en el tiempo, comparando solo cantidades comparables, **so that** sepamos dónde nos conviene comprar cada producto sin subir los datos a ningún servidor.

<objective>
Make a content correction count (D-13, D-14, D-15, D-28): the reducers and store actions that save, replace, remove and restore a correction per member within the cap, the precedence that puts a correction first for piece-unit purchases, the comparison and the hook recomputing with it, and the per-member content rows the content table of Plan 05-11 shows.

Purpose: on the real sample most Walmart descriptions carry no size (RESEARCH Pitfall 7); a stored correction is what turns them into real comparisons. After this plan a correction loaded from a settings file already changes every price; Plan 05-11 adds the section and the editor to capture one.
Output: correction reducers and store actions, content precedence with corrections, the corrections argument of the comparison, `members.ts`, the hook wiring and their tests.
</objective>
