## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** pedir a DeepSeek que agrupe como "mismo producto" los conceptos con descripciones distintas entre tiendas y que sugiera categorías, revisando cada sugerencia antes de aplicarla, **so that** nada cambia sin mi revisión y a DeepSeek solo llegan descripciones de conceptos y nombres de proveedor, por una ruta protegida.

<objective>
Turn on the product level of the category precedence (CAT-04, D-16): the user gives a product a category in «Mis productos» and the Dashboard, the filters and the Excel (with the «Del producto» origin) follow it, below concept rules and above the supplier; deleting a category carries the products along.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4. The delete-category change ships in the same plan as the Select so no stored product can ever point at a deleted category (that would make the stored settings unreadable on the next load). The Excel origin case ships here because the exhaustive origin switch stops compiling as soon as the new source exists (planning probe, 2026-10-19: conceptos.ts is the only file that breaks). The concept-panel label and the delete-dialog and usage texts that name products are Plan 04-09.
Output: resolver with `"producto"`, `setProductCategory`, the Categoría column, the Excel origin value, and products-aware delete, restore and usage.
</objective>
