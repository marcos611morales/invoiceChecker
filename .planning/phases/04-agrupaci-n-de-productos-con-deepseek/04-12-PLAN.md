## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** pedir a DeepSeek que agrupe como "mismo producto" los conceptos con descripciones distintas entre tiendas y que sugiera categorías, revisando cada sugerencia antes de aplicarla, **so that** nada cambia sin mi revisión y a DeepSeek solo llegan descripciones de conceptos y nombres de proveedor, por una ruta protegida.

<objective>
Complete the card actions of LLM-04: join a card with another suggestion or with an accepted product («Unir con…»), and split checked members into a new card («Separar en producto nuevo»), each undoable.

Purpose: DeepSeek will sometimes split one product in two or join two products in one; the user fixes that before accepting (ROADMAP criterion 2: aceptar, renombrar, separar, unir o rechazar).
Output: merge, turn-into-add and split reducers with undo, member checkboxes, the two buttons.
</objective>

- `turnIntoAddCard(queue, id, productId): { next; undo } | null`.
- `splitSuggestion(queue, id, keys: readonly string[], newId: string): { next; undo } | null` (null unless 1 <= keys present in the card < members).
- `undoQueueChange(queue, undo): PendingQueue` (removes createdId and puts the saved cards back at their indexes; members now pending elsewhere are left out).
