## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** pedir a DeepSeek que agrupe como "mismo producto" los conceptos con descripciones distintas entre tiendas y que sugiera categorías, revisando cada sugerencia antes de aplicarla, **so that** nada cambia sin mi revisión y a DeepSeek solo llegan descripciones de conceptos y nombres de proveedor, por una ruta protegida.

<objective>
Give the user the editable list of accepted products (D-14): see every product with its members and suppliers, rename it, delete it, and take out or move a member, each with «Deshacer», with freed descriptions going back to the free set (D-15).

Purpose: without this list an accepted product is invisible and cannot be fixed. The category column of the same table, which turns on the product level of CAT-04, is Plan 04-06.
Output: the product reducers with undo snapshots, store actions, `ProductList`, `ProductMembers`, `ProductPicker`, and the empty state of the tab.
</objective>
