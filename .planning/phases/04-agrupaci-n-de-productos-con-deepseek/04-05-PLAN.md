## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** pedir a DeepSeek que agrupe como "mismo producto" los conceptos con descripciones distintas entre tiendas y que sugiera categorías, revisando cada sugerencia antes de aplicarla, **so that** nada cambia sin mi revisión y a DeepSeek solo llegan descripciones de conceptos y nombres de proveedor, por una ruta protegida.

<objective>
Let the user accept a suggestion card and keep the product: the pure accept reducer over the version-3 settings of Plan 04-03, its store action, and «Aceptar» on the cards with its errors, toast, focus move and announcement.

Purpose: LLM-04 starts here (nothing applies without review) and ROADMAP criterion 2 (accepted products survive a reload). It runs in parallel with Plan 04-04, which touches only server, script and lint files.
Output: `src/domain/settings/products.ts`, the `acceptProduct` store action, `removeProduct` on the queue store, «Aceptar» on the cards.
</objective>
