## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** pedir a DeepSeek que agrupe como "mismo producto" los conceptos con descripciones distintas entre tiendas y que sugiera categorías, revisando cada sugerencia antes de aplicarla, **so that** nada cambia sin mi revisión y a DeepSeek solo llegan descripciones de conceptos y nombres de proveedor, por una ruta protegida.

<objective>
Show where a line's category comes from when the product decides it: «· del producto {nombre}» in the concept panel of the Facturas table, and the Categorías texts and the delete dialog that count products.

Purpose: D-16 asks for the product origin in grey next to each concept; the delete dialog of Plan 04-08 already moves products correctly and now says so and lets the user choose where they go. Split from Plan 04-08 to keep each plan under the per-plan file budget.
Output: `ConceptRow.productName`, the product branch of `ConceptCategorySelect`, the three-part `deleteCategoryBody` and `usageCaption`, `conceptCategoryProductAria`.
</objective>
