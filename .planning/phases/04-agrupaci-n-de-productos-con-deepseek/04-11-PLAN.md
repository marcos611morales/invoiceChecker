## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** pedir a DeepSeek que agrupe como "mismo producto" los conceptos con descripciones distintas entre tiendas y que sugiera categorías, revisando cada sugerencia antes de aplicarla, **so that** nada cambia sin mi revisión y a DeepSeek solo llegan descripciones de conceptos y nombres de proveedor, por una ruta protegida.

<objective>
Keep the review queue across reloads (D-24, D-30): a persisted store under its own key and version, validated with zod, with the shared storage adapter, the storage notices, the tab counter, and cards that still render without loaded invoices.

Purpose: LLM-05 (a failure or a reload keeps what already arrived) and ROADMAP criterion 2 for pending work. The schema defines the category-suggestion rows now (filled by Plan 04-18), so the stored contract does not change again inside this phase.
Output: `createSafeStorage`, the status-store factory, the persisted `useSuggestionsStore`, `pendingQueueSchema` and `resolvePendingQueue`, the Alerts and the counter.
</objective>
