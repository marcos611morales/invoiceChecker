## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** pedir a DeepSeek que agrupe como "mismo producto" los conceptos con descripciones distintas entre tiendas y que sugiera categorías, revisando cada sugerencia antes de aplicarla, **so that** nada cambia sin mi revisión y a DeepSeek solo llegan descripciones de conceptos y nombres de proveedor, por una ruta protegida.

<objective>
Let the user accept a suggestion card and keep the product: extend the persisted settings to version 3 (products and «No agrupar» marks, D-13) with a total migration from version 2, add the pure accept reducer, and wire «Aceptar» on the cards with its errors, toast, focus move and announcement.

Purpose: LLM-04 starts here (nothing applies without review) and ROADMAP criterion 2 (accepted products survive a reload). D-09 and D-13 are user decisions rated one-way; they are cited, not re-gated (orchestrator note 3). The backup file keeps writing version 1 until Plan 04-07 adds file version 2.
Output: settings model, schema, migration and store at version 3; `src/domain/settings/products.ts`; «Aceptar» on the cards.
</objective>
