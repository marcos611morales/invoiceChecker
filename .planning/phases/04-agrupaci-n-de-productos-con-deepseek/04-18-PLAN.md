## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** pedir a DeepSeek que agrupe como "mismo producto" los conceptos con descripciones distintas entre tiendas y que sugiera categorías, revisando cada sugerencia antes de aplicarla, **so that** nada cambia sin mi revisión y a DeepSeek solo llegan descripciones de conceptos y nombres de proveedor, por una ruta protegida.

<objective>
Deliver LLM-02 end to end up to the review: «Sugerir categorías» builds the payload of suppliers and products without category, shows it in the send dialog, sends it through a second session-protected route to DeepSeek, and lands one row per supplier or product in «Sugerencias de categoría» (read-only until Plan 04-19 adds the per-row actions).

Purpose: two routes on one server module (RESEARCH OQ5, Claude's discretion: different schemas and max_tokens); the runner, queue store and dialog of the grouping flow are reused. The table with Aceptar and Rechazar is split into Plan 04-19 to keep each plan under the per-plan file budget.
Output: categorize contracts, prompt and route, client call, targets and batches, queue rows, the dialog variant, the second button and the read-only rows.
</objective>
