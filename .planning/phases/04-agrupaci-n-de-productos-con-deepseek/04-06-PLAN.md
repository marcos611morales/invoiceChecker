## Phase Goal

**As a** dueño, socio o contador del Coworking Café, **I want to** pedir a DeepSeek que agrupe como "mismo producto" los conceptos con descripciones distintas entre tiendas y que sugiera categorías, revisando cada sugerencia antes de aplicarla, **so that** nada cambia sin mi revisión y a DeepSeek solo llegan descripciones de conceptos y nombres de proveedor, por una ruta protegida.

<objective>
Turn on the product level of the category precedence (CAT-04, D-16): the user gives a product a category in «Mis productos» and the Dashboard, the filters, the concept panel and the Excel follow it, below concept rules and above the supplier; deleting a category carries the products along.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4. The delete-category change ships in the same plan as the Select so no stored product can ever point at a deleted category (that would make the stored settings unreadable on the next load).
Output: resolver with `"producto"`, `setProductCategory`, the Categoría column, the concept-panel label, the Excel origin value and the products-aware delete, restore and usage.
</objective>
